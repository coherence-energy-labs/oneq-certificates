## Method and limits

This audit was done by Claude and five sub-agents on 6–7 November 2026, against the four PDFs you sent and the public repository at tag v2026.11.05 (commit 0e1e9d9). It is not peer review: no human specialist has read these papers, and that remains the most valuable next step.

- **Mathematics:** every lemma, theorem and closed form in the four papers was re-derived by hand; Paper 2's window and fold proofs (WINDOW\_PROOF.md, FOLD\_PROOF.md) were sent to a separate referee agent.
- **Numbers:** about 160 printed figures recomputed by script (exact Clopper–Pearson, exact McNemar, sums, rates, closed forms, thresholds), every mismatch traced to its source macro.
